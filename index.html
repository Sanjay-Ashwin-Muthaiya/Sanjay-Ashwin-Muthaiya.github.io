Build performance

Production builds about 87% faster with Rspack

I benchmarked Webpack 5 against Rspack on the Contacts codebase, comparing build time, raw bundle size and Gzip/Brotli output, then migrated the production build. The transpiler moved from Babel and ts-loader to SWC, and CI builds dropped from about 30 seconds to under 10.

RspackSWCWebpack 5CI/CD
Webpack 529.52 s
Rspack3.83 s
Brotli compression stayed about the same (83.4% vs 83.6%), so end users download the same amount.
